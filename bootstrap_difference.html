Centered Bootstrap Test: Difference Between Two Means

Enter a group label and a numeric value on each row. The app subtracts each group’s mean from its values, then resamples within each group. This creates a bootstrap distribution of differences under the null hypothesis that the group means are equal. Group 1 is the first label encountered; the plotted difference is Group 1 − Group 2.

  1. Paste two columns from a spreadsheet or enter data in the table.
  2. Select an alternative, then run the test.
  3. The chart shades the simulated null results at least as extreme as the observed difference. Their proportion estimates the p-value.
