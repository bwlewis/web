Bootstrapping the Mean

This bootstrap produces estimates of the mean by resampling data—randomly sampling data with replacement. The number of bootstrap replications is fixed at 10,000.
  1. Paste data from Excel in the cell below by pressing CTRL+v .
    Or, you can edit data in the cell directly and press CTRL+v to add more cells. Press the TAB key or SHIFT + TAB to navigate between ceels.
  2. Once you've entered some data press the "Plot bootstrapped mean histogram" button to create a histogram of the bootstrapped means.
  3. Change the "Bin width" slider to adjust histogram bin width.
