Null-centered, non-parametric bootstrap test for a single mean

This app shifts the entered data so its mean equals the null mean μ₀, then resamples from the shifted data. Click the histogram at the observed sample mean to calculate the p-value.
  1. Paste data from Excel into the cells below, or edit the cells directly. Press TAB or SHIFT+TAB to move between cells.
  2. Enter the null mean and select the alternative hypothesis.
  3. Click “Plot null-bootstrap means,” then click the histogram at the observed sample mean.
