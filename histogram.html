Histograms

A histogram is a visual representation of the distribution of quantitative data. To construct a histogram, the first step is to "bin" (or "bucket") the range of values—divide the entire range of values into a series of intervals—and then count how many values fall into each bin. The bins are usually consecutive, non-overlapping intervals, typically of equal size. Histograms give a rough sense of the underlying distribution of the data.   —Adapted from Wikipedia
